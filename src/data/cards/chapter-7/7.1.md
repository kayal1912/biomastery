Describe a balanced diet:

<!-- answer -->

A diet containing all essential nutrients in the correct proportions
<!-- card -->

What is scurvy?

<!-- answer -->

a disease caused by the lack of Vitamin C
<!-- card -->

What are the symptoms of scurvy?

<!-- answer -->

- bleeding gums
- loose teeth
- poor wound healing
- fatigue / weakness
- bruising easily

<!-- card -->

What is rickets?

<!-- answer -->

a disease caused by the lack of Vitamin D
<!-- card -->

What are the symptoms of rickets?

<!-- answer -->

- soft/weak bones
- bone deformities (bowed legs)
- bone pain
- stunted growth in children
