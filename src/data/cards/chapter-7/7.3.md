Describe physical digestion:

<!-- answer -->

the breakdown of food into smaller pieces without chemical change to the food
molecules
<!-- card -->

What is the role of physical digestion?

<!-- answer -->

it increases the surface area of food for the action of enzymes in chemical digestion
<!-- card -->

What are the types of human teeth?

<!-- answer -->

- incisor
- canine
- premolar
- molar

<!-- card -->

What is the role of bile in physical digestion?

<!-- answer -->

it emulsifies fats and oils into small droplets to increase surface area for chemical
digestion by enzymes
