Where are nutrients absorbed?

<!-- answer -->

in the small intestine
<!-- card -->

Where is water absorbed?

<!-- answer -->

- most of the water is absorbed from the small intestine
- but some is absorbed from the colon

<!-- card -->

What is the significance of villi and microvilli:

<!-- answer -->

- they increase the internal surface of the small intestine for diffusion
- they have thin epithelium to decrease diffusion rate
- they have a dense network of blood capillaries for good blood supply
- they have lacteals to absorb fat
