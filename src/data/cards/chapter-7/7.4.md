Describe chemical digestion:

<!-- answer -->

the breakdown of large insoluble molecules into small soluble molecules
<!-- card -->

What is the role of chemical digestion?

<!-- answer -->

it produces small soluble molecules that can be absorbed into the blood
(large insoluble molecules are too big to be absorbed)
<!-- card -->

Describe the functions of the following enzymes:

<!-- answer -->

- amylase: breaks down starch to simple reducing sugars
- proteases: breaks down proteins into amino acids
- lipase: breaks down fats and oils to fatty acids and glycerol

<!-- card -->

What is the function of hydrochloric acid in gastric juice?

<!-- answer -->

- kills harmful microorganisms
- provides an acidic pH optimum for enzyme activity

<!-- card -->

Outline the digestion of starch:

<!-- answer -->

- first amylase breaks down starch to maltose
- then maltase breaks down maltose to glucose

<!-- card -->

Outline the digestion of protein:

<!-- answer -->

- prepsin breaks down protein in the acidic conditions of the stomach
- trypsin breaks down protein in the alkaline conditions of the small intestine

<!-- card -->

What is bile?

<!-- answer -->

- it is an alkaline mixture that neutralises the acidic mixture of food and gastric juices
entering the duodenum from the stomach
- to produce a suitable pH for enzyme action
