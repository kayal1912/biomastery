---
Describe ingestion:

<!-- answer -->

the taking of substances into the body
<!-- card -->

Describe digestion:

<!-- answer -->

the break down of food
<!-- card -->

Describe absorption:

<!-- answer -->

the movement of nutrients from the intestines into the blood
<!-- card -->

Describe assimilation:

<!-- answer -->

the uptake and use of nutrients by cells where they become a part of the cell
<!-- card -->

Describe Egestion:

<!-- answer -->

the removal of undigested food from the body as faeces
