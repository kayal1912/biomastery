Describe genetic modification:

<!-- answer -->

changing the genetic material of an organism by removing, changing or inserting
individual genes
<!-- card -->

What are some examples of genetic modification?

<!-- answer -->

- insertion of human genes into bacteria to produce human proteins
- insertion of genes into crop plants to confer resistance to herbicides
- insertion of genes into crop plants to confer resistance to insect pests
- insertion of genes into crop plants to improve nutritional qualities

<!-- card -->

Outline the process of genetic modification using bacteria and human genes:

<!-- answer -->

- the DNA making up a human gene is isolated using a restriction enzyme forming sticky
ends
- the bacterial plasmid DNA is cut with the same restriction enzyme, forming
complementary sticky ends
- the human DNA is inserted into the bacterial plasmid DNA using DNA lipase to form a
recombinant plasmid
- the recombinant plasmid is placed back into the bacteria
- the bacteria containing the recombinant plasmid multiplies
- the human gene is expressed in the bacteria and the human protein is produced

<!-- card -->

What are the advantages and disadvantages of genetically modifying crops?

<!-- answer -->

(a)Advantages:
- Increased yield
- Resistance to pests/diseases meaning less pesticide use
- Tolerance to harsh conditions (like drought)
- Improved nutritional value
(b)Disadvantages:
- Possible harm to ecosystems (like gene transfer to wild plants)
- Reduction in biodiversity
- Ethical concerns
- Uncertain long-term health effects
