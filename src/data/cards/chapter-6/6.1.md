Define photosynthesis:

<!-- answer -->

the process by which plants synthesise carbohydrates from raw materials using energy
from light
<!-- card -->

State the word equation for photosynthesis:

<!-- answer -->

carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen
(in the presence of light and chlorophyll)
<!-- card -->

What are the carbohydrates made in photosynthesis used/stored as?

<!-- answer -->

- starch: energy store
- cellulose: build cell walls
- glucose: respiration to provide energy
- sucrose: transport in the phloem
- nectar: attract insects for pollination

<!-- card -->

Why are nitrate ions important?

<!-- answer -->

needed to make amino acids
<!-- card -->

Why are magnesium ions important?

<!-- answer -->

needed to make chlorophyll
