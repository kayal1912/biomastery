Describe osmosis (3 marks):

<!-- answer -->

- net movement of water molecules
- from a region of higher water potential (dilute solution) to a region of lower water
potential (concentration solution)
- though a partially permeable membrane

<!-- card -->

Explain the effects of immersing plant cells in the following solutions:

<!-- answer -->

(a)Hypotonic:
- water enters the cell
- cell becomes turgid and turgor pressure increases
- cell does not burst due to presence of an inelastic cell wall
(b)Hypertonic
- water leaves the cell
- cell becomes flaccid and becomes plasmolysed
- turgor pressure is lost
