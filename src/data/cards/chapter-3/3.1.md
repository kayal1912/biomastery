Describe diffusion (2 marks):

<!-- answer -->

- the net movement of particles from a region of their higher concentration to a region of
their lower concentration (down a concentration gradient)
- as a result of their random movement

<!-- card -->

How do these factors influence diffusion:

<!-- answer -->

(a)Surface area - large surface area increases diffusion rate
(b)Temperature - high temperature increases diffusion rate
(c) Concentration gradient - steep concentration gradient increases diffusion rate
(d)Distance - lower distance of diffusion increases diffusion rate
