State the functions of the following structures:

<!-- answer -->

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Petal | Large and coloured to attract insects |
| Sepal | Protects the flower while in bud |
| Stamen | Male reproductive part of the flower, made up of anther and filament |
| Anther | Contains pollen sacs in which pollen grains are formed |
| Filament | Supports the anther |
| Carpel | Female reproductive part of the flower, made up of stigma, style, and ovary |
| Stigma | A sticky surface that receives pollen during fertilisation |
| Style | Links the stigma to the ovary through which pollen tubes grow |
| Ovary | Contains ovules |
| Ovule | Contains a haploid nucleus, which develops into a seed when fertilised |

<!-- card -->

List the features of wind and insect pollinated flowers:

<!-- answer -->

| Wind | Insect |
|---|---|
| Small or absent petals/flowers | Large, colourful petals to attract pollinators |
| Anther and filament hang freely outside the flower | Anthers and filament are inside the flower |
| Feathery stigma hangs freely outside the flower | Stigma is located inside the flower |
| Small, smooth, lightweight pollen | Large/heavy, hooked/spiky pollen |

<!-- card -->

Describe pollination:

<!-- answer -->

the transfer of pollen grains from an anther to a stigma
<!-- card -->

When does fertilisation happen?

<!-- answer -->

fertilisation occurs when a pollen nucleus fuses with a nucleus in an ovule
<!-- card -->

What environmental conditions are needed for germination?

<!-- answer -->

- water
- oxygen
- suitable temperature
