Describe sexual reproduction:

<!-- answer -->

a process involving the fusion of the nuclei of two gametes to form a zygote and the
production of offspring that are genetically different from each other
<!-- card -->

Describe fertilisation:

<!-- answer -->

the fusion of the nuclei of gametes
<!-- card -->

Describe the nuclei of gametes and zygotes:

<!-- answer -->

- a gamete has a haploid nucleus
- a zygote has diploid nucleus

<!-- card -->

What are the advantages and disadvantages of sexual reproduction?

<!-- answer -->

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| There is variation among the offspring, so adaptation and evolution are likely | Two parents are usually needed |
| New varieties can be created which may have disease resistance | |
| In plants, the seeds produced are dispersed, reducing competition | |
