Describe the functions of the following male reproductive structures:

<!-- answer -->

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Penis | Can become firm to insert into the vagina during sexual intercourse in order to transfer sperm |
| Prostate gland | Adds fluid and nutrients to sperm to form semen |
| Scrotum | A sac that holds the testes outside the body, keeping them cooler than body temperature |
| Sperm duct | Muscular tube that links the testis to the urethra to allow the passage of semen containing sperm |
| Testis | Male gonad that produces sperm |
| Urethra | Passes semen containing sperm through the penis; also carries urine from the bladder |

<!-- card -->

Describe the functions of the following female reproductive structures:

<!-- answer -->

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Cervix | A ring of muscle separating the vagina from the uterus |
| Ovary | Female gonad; contains follicles in which ova are produced |
| Oviduct | Carries an ovum to the uterus with propulsion provided by tiny cilia in the wall; site of fertilisation |
| Uterus | Where the fetus develops |
| Vagina | Receives the male penis during sexual intercourse |

<!-- card -->

Describe the functions of the following structures involved in pregnancy:

<!-- answer -->

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Umbilical cord | Keeps maternal and fetal blood separate; transfers oxygen and dissolved nutrients from mother to fetus; transfers excretory waste from fetus to mother; transfers antibodies/passive immunity to fetus |
| Placenta | Secretes progesterone; forms a barrier to toxins |
| Amniotic sac | Acts as a barrier against infections; produces and holds fluid; encloses the fetus |
| Amniotic fluid | Absorbs shock; regulates temperature; allows lung development; allows the fetus to move so muscles and bones develop |

<!-- card -->

Describe fertilisation:

<!-- answer -->

The fusion of the nuclei from a male gamete (sperm) and a female gamete (egg cell).
<!-- card -->

Explain the adaptive features of sperm:

<!-- answer -->

- **Flagellum:** produces flicking movements to propel sperm and allow it to swim to the egg cell for fertilisation.
- **Mitochondria:** respires aerobically to release energy for movement.
- **Acrosome:** has enzymes which digest the jelly coat of an egg cell to allow the sperm nucleus to enter for fertilisation.

<!-- card -->

Explain the adaptive features of egg cells:

<!-- answer -->

- **Energy stores:** provide energy for cell division and early development of an embryo.
- **Jelly coat:** changes after fertilisation to prevent more than one sperm entering.
