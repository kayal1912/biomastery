Describe sexually transmitted infections:

<!-- answer -->

an infection that is transmitted through sexual contact
<!-- card -->

What can HIV do?

<!-- answer -->

the infection may lead to AIDS
<!-- card -->

Describe the transmission of HIV:

<!-- answer -->

- blood transfusions
- unprotected sex
- sharing needles
- breast feeding
- during birth

<!-- card -->

How is the spread of STIs controlled?

<!-- answer -->

- abstinence
- barrier method of contraception
- clean/do not share needles
- blood screening (with donors)
- HIV screening (with partners)
- bottle feeding
- sterilise medical equipment
