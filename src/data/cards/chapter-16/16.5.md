Describe the role of:

<!-- answer -->

- testosterone in development of secondary sexual characteristics:
- shoulders broaden
- voice deepens
- sexual organs enlarge
- facial hair grows
- growth of pubic hair
- increase in muscle/bone mass
- production/release of sperm
- oestrogen in development of secondary sexual characteristics:
- breasts grow
- hips widen
- pubic hair grows
- start of menstrual cycle

<!-- card -->

Describe the menstrual cycle:

<!-- answer -->

- Day 1-6: At the start of the cycle, the lining of the uterus breaks down and is shed
(menstruation)
- Day 6-13: The uterus lining then repairs and thickens
- Day 14: In the ovary, an egg develops and is released (ovulation)
- Day 15- 28: The uterus lining becomes thicker and develops more blood vessels ready
for implantation
- If fertilisation does not occur, the lining breaks down again

<!-- card -->

Where are the following hormones produced?

<!-- answer -->

(a)oestrogen - ovaries
(b)progesterone - corpus luteum in the ovary; placenta during pregnancy

<!-- card -->

Explain the roles of the following hormones:

<!-- answer -->

(a)FSH (follicle stimulating hormone)
- Stimulates development/growth/maturation of follicles in the ovary
- Stimulates development/growth/maturation of egg cells
- Stimulates production/secretion of oestrogen (from ovary)
(b)LH (lutenising hormone)
- Stimulates ovulation (release of egg cell)
- Stimulates (corpus luteum) secretion of progesterone
(c) Oestrogen
- Thickens the blood lining of the uterus
(d)Progesterone
- Maintains blood lining of uterus during pregnancy for implantation
- Inhibits FSH and LH secretion
