Describe asexual reproduction:

<!-- answer -->

a process resulting in the production of genetically identical offspring from one parent
<!-- card -->

What are the advantages and disadvantages of asexual reproduction?

<!-- answer -->

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| No mate is needed | Little to no variation, so unlikely to evolve or adapt to changing environments |
| No gametes are needed | If the parent has no disease resistance, offspring will not either |
| All the good characteristics of a parent are passed down to offspring | Lack of dispersal can lead to competition for nutrients, water, and light |
| No dispersal, so offspring grow in the same favourable conditions as the parent plant | |
