Describe variation:

<!-- answer -->

the differences between individuals of the same species
<!-- card -->

What is continuous variation?

<!-- answer -->

- characteristics as a result of a range of phenotypes between two extremes (like height
and body mass)
- caused by both genes and environment

<!-- card -->

What is discontinuous variation?

<!-- answer -->

- characteristics as a result of a limited number of phenotypes with no intermediates (like
ABO blood groups, seed shape and seed color in peas)
- caused by genes only

<!-- card -->

Describe mutation:

<!-- answer -->

a genetic change
<!-- card -->

How are new alleles formed?

<!-- answer -->

mutation
<!-- card -->

What factors increase the rate of mutation?

<!-- answer -->

- ionising radiation
- some chemicals

<!-- card -->

Describe gene mutation:

<!-- answer -->

a random change in the base sequence of DNA
<!-- card -->

What are the sources of genetic variation?

<!-- answer -->

- mutation
- meiosis
- random mating
- random fertilisation
