Describe the process of natural selection:

<!-- answer -->

- there is genetic variation within populations
- there is selection pressure (change in the environment)
- there is a struggle for survival and competition for resources
- individuals with advantageous features are more likely to survive and reproduce
- these (advantageous) individuals pass on their alleles to the next generation

<!-- card -->

Describe the process of selective breeding (4 marks - question will be asked through an example):

<!-- answer -->

- humans select individuals with desirable features
- these individuals are crossed to produce the next generation
- the offspring with the desired features are selected
- the process is repeated over many generations

<!-- card -->

Describe adaptation:

<!-- answer -->

the process, resulting from natural selection, by which populations become more suited
to their environment over many generations
<!-- card -->

What is an example of natural selection?

<!-- answer -->

- development of strains of antibiotic resistant bacteria
- a mutation occurs
- some bacteria become resistant to antibiotics
- the antibiotics kill all the non resistant bacteria
- the resistant bacteria survive and reproduce
- they pass on their genes for resistance to the next generation of offspring

<!-- card -->

What are the differences between natural and artificial selection?

<!-- answer -->

| Natural | Artificial |
|---|---|
| Features are adaptive | Humans choose desired features |
| Caused by the environment | Selection is not influenced by the environment |
| Competition between individuals | Humans choose individuals for economic/aesthetic reasons |
| Slower | Faster |
| Maintains variation | Decreases variation |
