Describe an adaptive feature:

<!-- answer -->

an inherited feature that helps an organism to survive and reproduce in its environment
<!-- card -->

What are the adaptive features of:

<!-- answer -->

(a)Hydrophytes
- aerenchyma: provides buoyancy
- thin cuticle: no need to prevent water loss
- many stomata on upper surface only: exposed to air, prevents water from entering the
leaf, allows for high rate of transpiration
(b)Xerophytes
- thick waxy cuticle: reduced water loss
- sunken stomata: traps humid air, reduces transpiration
- leaves reduced to spines: less surface area for transpiration
- rolled leaves: traps moisture, reducing transpiration
- extensive roots: maximise water uptake
