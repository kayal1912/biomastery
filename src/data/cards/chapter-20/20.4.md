Describe a sustainable resource:

<!-- answer -->

one which is produced as rapidly as it is removed from the environment so that it does
not run out
<!-- card -->

Explain why organisms become endangered or extinct:

<!-- answer -->

- climate change
- habitat destruction
- disease
- hunting/poaching
- overharvesting
- pollution
- introduction of new species
- lack of mates

<!-- card -->

How can endangered (plant) species be conserved (4-6 marks)?

<!-- answer -->

- monitoring and protecting species and habitats (syllabus)
- education (syllabus)
- captive breeding programs (syllabus)
- seed banks (syllabus)
- remove vectors of disease (mark scheme)
- remove predators (mark scheme)
- protecting/preserving/making new habitats (mark scheme)
- preserve embryos (mark scheme)
- ban hunting (mark scheme)

<!-- card -->

How can forests be conserved (4 marks)?

<!-- answer -->

- education
- protected areas
- quotas
- replanting

<!-- card -->

How can fish stocks be conserved (6 marks)?

<!-- answer -->

- education
- closed seasons
- protected areas
- controlled net types
- controlled mesh size
- quotas
- monitoring the populations
- make harvesting illegal

<!-- card -->

What are the risks to a species if its population size decreases?

<!-- answer -->

- reduction in genetic variation
- unable to find mates
- inbreeding
- increased frequency of recessive alleles
- risk of extinction
