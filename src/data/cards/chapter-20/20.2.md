Describe biodiversity:

<!-- answer -->

the number of different species that live in an area
<!-- card -->

Describe reasons for habitat destruction:

<!-- answer -->

- increased area for housing, crop plant production, livestock production
- extraction of natural resources/mining
- freshwater and marine pollution

<!-- card -->

What are the undesirable effects of deforestation:

<!-- answer -->

- reducing biodiversity
- extinction
- loss of soil
- flooding
- increase of atmospheric carbon dioxide
