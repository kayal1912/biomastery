How have humans increased food production?

<!-- answer -->

- agricultural machinery: use larger areas of land and improve efficiency
- chemical fertilisers: improve yields
- insecticides: improve quality and yield
- herbicides: reduce competition with weeds
- selective breeding: improve production by crop plants and livestock

<!-- card -->

Describe the advantages and disadvantages of large scale monocultures:

<!-- answer -->

(a)advantages
- high yield
- efficient
- easier management
(b)disadvantages
- lack of biodiversity
- habitat loss
- extinction of species
- diseases spread easily
- flooding/droughts
- depletes soil nutrients
- pollution by pesticides

<!-- card -->

Describe the advantages and disadvantages of intensive livestock production:

<!-- answer -->

(a)advantages
- more economical
- less space used
- more food produced
- easier to monitor
- efficient
- protection from predators
(b)disadvantages
- habitat loss
- overuse of antibiotics
- animal welfare concerns
- eutrophication/water pollution
- higher greenhouse gas emissions
- increased risk of disease
