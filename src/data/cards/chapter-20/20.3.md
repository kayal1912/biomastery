What are the effects of non biodegradable plastics in both aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems?

<!-- answer -->

- Do not decompose (accumulates in environment)
- Harm animals (ingestion can lead to choking/starvation)
- Entanglement (can injure or kill organisms)
- Pollute habitats (land and water)
- Can enter food chains and bioaccumulate

<!-- card -->

Explain the eutrophication of water:

<!-- answer -->

- increased availability of nitrate and other ions
- increased growth of producers
- increased decomposition after death of producers
- increased aerobic respiration by decomposers
- reduction in dissolved oxygen
- death of organisms requiring dissolved oxygen
