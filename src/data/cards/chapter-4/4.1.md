What chemical elements make up a carbohydrate molecule?

<!-- answer -->

- carbon
- hydrogen
- oxygen

<!-- card -->

What chemicals make up a fat molecule?

<!-- answer -->

- carbon
- hydrogen
- (relatively low) oxygen

<!-- card -->

What chemicals make up a protein molecule?

<!-- answer -->

- carbon
- hydrogen
- oxygen
- nitrogen

<!-- card -->

What small molecules are the following large molecules made from?

<!-- answer -->

(a)Starch, glycogen and cellulose - glucose
(b)proteins - amino acids
(c) fats and oils (lipids) - fatty acids and glycerol

<!-- card -->

What do these substances test?

<!-- answer -->

(a)Iodine - starch (color changes from yellow-brown to blue-black)
(b)Benedict’s solution - reducing sugars (heat mixture (1m); color changes from blue to
brick red (1m))
(c) Biuret - protein (color changes from blue to purple)
(d)Ethanol - fats and oils (shake substance with ethanol (1m); milky white emulsion
forms (1m))
(e)DCPIP - vitamin C (color changes from blue to colorless)

<!-- card -->

Describe the structure of a DNA molecule:

<!-- answer -->

- two strands coiled together to form a double helix
- each strand contains chemicals called bases
- bonds between pairs of bases hold the strands together
- A always pairs with T, C always pairs with G
