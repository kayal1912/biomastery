State the specific function of the following specialised cells:

<!-- answer -->

(a)Ciliates cells - movement of mucus in the trachea and bronchi
(b)Root hair cells - absorption
(c) Palisade mesophyll cells - photosynthesis
(d)Neurones - conduction of electrical impulses
(e)Red blood cells - transport of oxygen
(f) Sperm and egg cells (gametes) - reproduction

<!-- card -->

Define cell:

<!-- answer -->

smallest basic unit of animal or plant
<!-- card -->

Define tissue:

<!-- answer -->

group of cells with a similar structure working together to perform a specific function
<!-- card -->

Define organ:

<!-- answer -->

a group of tissues with similar structures working together to perform a specific function
<!-- card -->

Define organ system:

<!-- answer -->

group of organs with related functions working together to perform a body function
<!-- card -->

Define organism:

<!-- answer -->

a living thing that has an organised structure, can react to stimuli, reproduce grow, adapt,
and maintain homeostasis
