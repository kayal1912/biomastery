Describe the structure of arteries, veins, and capillaries:

<!-- answer -->

| Feature | Artery | Vein | Capillary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relative thickness of wall | Thick walls | Thin walls | Very thin walls (only one cell thick) |
| Diameter of lumen | Narrow lumen | Wide lumen | Approximately one red blood cell wide |
| Valves | Absent | Present | Absent |

<!-- card -->

What is the function of capillaries?

<!-- answer -->

- Walls are one cell thick → short diffusion distance
- Form a large network (large surface area) → more exchange
- Slow blood flow → more time for diffusion
- Allow diffusion of oxygen and glucose into cells
- Allow diffusion of carbon dioxide and other wastes into blood
