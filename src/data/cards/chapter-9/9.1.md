Describe the circulatory system:

<!-- answer -->

a system of blood vessels with a pump and valves to ensure one way flow of blood
<!-- card -->

Explain the advantages of a double circulation (according to the mark scheme):

<!-- answer -->

- keeps oxygenated and deoxygenated blood separate
- ensures efficient supply of oxygen (to body)
- low pressure in pulmonary artery (to lungs)
- (to) prevent damage to capillaries in the lungs
- allows more time for gas exchange
- allows higher blood pressure to body
- allows filtration in kidneys
- maintain a high metabolic rate/rate of respiration
