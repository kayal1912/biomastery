What are the components of blood?

<!-- answer -->

- red blood cells
- white blood cells
- platelets
- plasma

<!-- card -->

State the functions of the following components of blood:

<!-- answer -->

(a)Red blood cells - transporting oxygen
(b)White blood cells - phagocytosis and antibody production
(c) Platelets - clotting
(d)Plasma - transport of blood cells, ions, nutrients, urea, hormones and carbon dioxide

<!-- card -->

What is the role of blood clotting?

<!-- answer -->

- prevent blood loss
- prevent entry of pathogens

<!-- card -->

State the function of:

<!-- answer -->

(a)Lymphocytes: antibody production
(b)Phagocytes: engulfing pathogens by phagocytosis

<!-- answer -->

(exact wording) the conversion of fibrinogen to fibrin to form a mesh
