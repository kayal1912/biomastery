Define a hormone?

<!-- answer -->

a chemical substance, produced by a gland and carried by the blood, which alters the
activity of one or more specific target organs
<!-- card -->

State what hormones these glands secrete:

<!-- answer -->

- adrenal glands: adrenaline
- pancreas: insulin
- testes: testosterone
- ovaries: oestrogen

<!-- card -->

Compare nervous and hormonal control:

<!-- answer -->

| Nervous | Hormonal |
|---|---|
| Fast transmission | Slow transmission |
| Short term effects | Long term effects |

<!-- card -->

Where is glucagon secreted?

the pancreas
