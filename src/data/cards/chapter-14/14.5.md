Describe gravitropism:

<!-- answer -->

a response in which parts of a plant grow towards or away from gravity
*positive gravitropism - downwards (towards gravity)
negative gravitropism - upwards (away from gravity)
<!-- card -->

Describe phototropism:

<!-- answer -->

a response in which parts of a plant grow towards or away from the direction of the light
source
<!-- card -->

Explain the role of auxin in controlling shoot growth ( for example towards light ):

<!-- answer -->

- auxin is made in the shoot tip
- auxin diffuses through the plant from the shoot tip (to the shady side of the plant)
- auxin is unequally distributed in response to light and gravity
- auxin stimulates cell elongation (the cells elongate on the shady side and the plant
bends toward light)
