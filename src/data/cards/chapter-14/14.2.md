Describe the functions of the following parts of the eye:

<!-- answer -->

(a)cornea - refracts light
(b)iris - controls how much light enters the pupil
(c) lens - focuses light on to the retina
(d)retina - contains light receptors, some sensitive to light of different colors
(e)optic nerve - carries impulses to the brain

<!-- card -->

Explain pupil reflex:

<!-- answer -->

(an automatic response to light intensity)
- in bright light
- the circular muscles of the iris contract and the radial muscles relax (they act
antagonistically)
- pupil size decreases / the iris widens
- allowing less light to enter the eye
- in dim light
- the radial muscles contract and the circular muscles relax
- pupil size increases
- allowing more light to enter the eye

<!-- card -->

Explain accommodation for viewing near and distant objects:

<!-- answer -->

(a)near objects (4 marks)
(i)
ciliary muscles contract
(ii)
tension in suspensory ligaments decreases
(iii)
lens becomes more convex/fatter
(iv)
refracting more light
(b)distant objects
(i)
ciliary muscles relax
(ii)
suspensory ligaments tighten
(iii)
suspensory ligaments pull on/stretch the lens
(iv)
lens becomes thinner/less convex
(v)
less light is refracted
