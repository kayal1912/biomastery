Describe the mammalian nervous system:

<!-- answer -->

(a)central nervous system (CNS) - brain and spinal chord
(b)peripheral nervous system (PNS) - nerves outside the brain and spinal chord

<!-- card -->

Describe a reflex arc:

<!-- answer -->

receptor (sense organ) → sensory neurone → relay neurone → motor neurone →
effector (muscles or glands)
<!-- card -->

Describe a synapse:

<!-- answer -->

a junction between two neurones
<!-- card -->

Describe the events at a synapse:

<!-- answer -->

- an impulse stimulates the release of neurotransmitter molecules from vesicles into the
synaptic gap
- the neurotransmitter molecules diffuse across the gap
- neurotransmitter molecules bind with receptor proteins on the membrane of the next
neurone
- an impulse is stimulated in the next neurone

<!-- card -->

What is the role of a synapse?

<!-- answer -->

synapses ensure that impulses travel in one direction only
