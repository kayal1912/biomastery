Describe homeostasis:

<!-- answer -->

the maintenance of a constant internal environment
<!-- card -->

What is the role of insulin?

<!-- answer -->

insulin decreases blood glucose concentration
<!-- card -->

Describe the control of blood glucose concentration:

<!-- answer -->

(a)High blood glucose levels
(i)
high glucose levels detected by the pancreas
(ii)
pancreas releases insulin
(iii)
insulin stimulates the liver to convert glucose into glycogen and store it
(iv)
therefore reducing the blood glucose levels to the set point
(b)Low blood glucose levels
(i)
low glucose levels detected by the pancreas
(ii)
pancreas releases glucagon
(iii)
glucagon stimulates the liver to convert glycogen back to glucose and release it
into the blood stream
(iv)
therefore increasing blood glucose levels to the set point

<!-- card -->

Outline the treatment of type 1 diabetes:

<!-- answer -->

- insulin injections
- regular monitoring of blood glucose levels
- regular exercise
- controlled diet
