Outline the functions of a nephron and the associated blood vessels:

<!-- answer -->

- glomerulus: filtration of water, glucose, urea and ions from the blood (not protein as
protein is too big to filter through - so there is no protein present in the nephron)
- nephron: reabsorption of all of the glucose, some of the ions, and most of the water back
into the blood
- forming urine containing urea, excess water, and excess ions (in the collecting duct)

Describe deamination:

<!-- answer -->

the removal of the nitrogen containing part of amino acids to form urea
