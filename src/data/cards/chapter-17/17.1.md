What are chromosomes:

<!-- answer -->

chromosomes are made of DNA which contains genetic information in the form of genes
<!-- card -->

Define gene:

<!-- answer -->

a length of DNA that codes for protein
<!-- card -->

Define allele:

<!-- answer -->

an alternative form of a gene
<!-- card -->

Explain how protein is made:

<!-- answer -->

- the gene coding for the protein remains in the nucleus
- messenger RNA (mRNA) is a copy of a gene
- mRNA molecules are made in the nucleus and move to the cytoplasm
- the mRNA passes through ribosomes
- the ribosome assembles amino acids into protein molecules
- the specific sequence of amino acids is determined by the sequence of bases in the
mRNA

<!-- card -->

Why do body cells have different functions though they contain the same genes?

<!-- answer -->

- most cells in an organism contain the same genes
- but many genes in a particular cell are not expressed because the cell only makes the
specific proteins it needs

<!-- card -->

Describe haploid nucleus:

<!-- answer -->

a nucleus containing a single set of chromosomes
<!-- card -->

Describe diploid nucleus:

<!-- answer -->

a nucleus containing two sets of chromosomes
