Describe inheritance:

<!-- answer -->

the transmission of genetic information from generation to generation
<!-- card -->

Describe genotype:

<!-- answer -->

the genetic make-up of an organism
<!-- card -->

Describe phenotype:

<!-- answer -->

the observable features of an organism
<!-- card -->

Describe homozygous:

<!-- answer -->

having two identical alleles of a particular gene
<!-- card -->

Describe heterozygous:

<!-- answer -->

having two different alleles for a particular gene
<!-- card -->

Describe a dominant allele:

<!-- answer -->

an allele that is expressed if it is present in the genotype
<!-- card -->

Describe recessive allele:

<!-- answer -->

an allele that is only expressed when there is no dominant allele of the gene present in
the genotype
<!-- card -->

Describe codominance:

<!-- answer -->

A situation in which both alleles in heterozygous organisms contribute to the phenotype
<!-- card -->

Describe sex linked characteristic:

<!-- answer -->

- a feature in which the gene responsible is located on a sex chromosome
- this makes the characteristic more common in one sex than in the other
