Describe mitosis:

<!-- answer -->

nuclear division giving rise to genetically identical cells
<!-- card -->

What is the role of mitosis?

<!-- answer -->

- growth
- repair of damaged tissue
- replacement of cells
- asexual reproduction

<!-- card -->

What must happen before mitosis occurs?

<!-- answer -->

Exact replication of chromosomes
<!-- card -->

Describe stem cells:

<!-- answer -->

unspecialised cells that divide by mitosis to produce daughter cells that can become
specialised for specific functions
