What are the features of gas exchange surfaces in humans?

<!-- answer -->

- large surface area
- thin surface
- good blood supply
- good ventilation with air

<!-- card -->

State the function of cartilage in the trachea:

<!-- answer -->

- Supports / prevents collapse of trachea
- Forms incomplete rings around the trachea/bronchi
- Keeps airways open
- Reduces resistance to air movement
- Protects airways

<!-- card -->

How does physical activity increase breathing rate and depth:

<!-- answer -->

- an increased carbon dioxide concentration in the blood
- is detected by the brain
- leading to an increased rate and greater depth of breathing
- (to supply more oxygen for respiration and remove carbon dioxide)

<!-- card -->

What are the roles of the following in the breathing system:

<!-- answer -->

- goblet cells: produce mucus
- mucus: trap dust and pathogens entering the airways
- ciliated cells: move in wave like motions to sweep mucus towards the oesophagus
(where it is then swallowed and killed by stomach acid)
