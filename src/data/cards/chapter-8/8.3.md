What is transpiration?

<!-- answer -->

the loss of water vapour from leaves
<!-- card -->

What factors increase the rate of transpiration?

<!-- answer -->

- high temperature
- high wind speed
- low humidity

<!-- card -->

How does wilting occur?

<!-- answer -->

- more water is lost by transpiration than is taken up by osmosis
- the plant cells lose turgor pressure and become flaccid
- the leaves and stem droop, becoming wilted
