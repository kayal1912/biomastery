What is the function of xylem and phloem?

<!-- answer -->

- xylem: transport of water and mineral ions; support
- phloem: transport of sucrose and amino acids

<!-- card -->

Name the features of xylem:

<!-- answer -->

- Thick walls with waterproof lignin (strengthens)
- No cell contents (dead cells)
- Cells joined end to end with no cross walls to form a long continuous tube
