:core[Describe a pathogen:]

- :core[a pathogen is a disease causing organism ]

:core[Describe a transmissible disease:]

- :core[a disease in which the pathogen can be passed from one host to another]

:core[How can pathogens be transmitted?]

1. :core[by direct contact - through blood and other body fluids ]
2. :core[indirectly - contaminated surfaces, food, animals and air ]

:core[Describe body defences:]

- :core[skin ]
- :core[hairs in nose ]
- :core[mucus ]
- :core[stomach acid ]
- :core[white blood cells ]

:core[Explain the importance of the following in controlling the spread of disease:]

- :core[Clean water supply:]

- :core[Prevents water-borne diseases (like cholera & typhoid) ]
- :core[Stops people ingesting pathogens in contaminated water ]

- :core[Hygienic food preparation:]

- :core[Kills pathogens through proper cooking ]
- :core[Prevents cross-contamination ]

- :core[Good personal hygiene:]

- :core[Washing hands removes pathogens → reduces person-to-person transmission ]
- :core[Prevents spread via touch, food, surfaces ]

- :core[Waste disposal:]

- :core[Removes sources of pathogens ]
- :core[Prevents breeding of vectors (like flies, rats) that spread disease ]

- :core[Sewage treatment:]

- :core[Removes/kills pathogens in human waste ]
- :core[Prevents contamination of water supplies and environment]

:supplement[What is active immunity?]

- :supplement[defense against a pathogen by antibody production in the body]
- :supplement[memory cells are produced]

:supplement[\\*a pathogen has its own antigens which have specific shapes]

:supplement[What are antibodies?]

- :supplement[proteins that bind to antigens leading to direct destruction of pathogens or marking of pathogens for destruction by phagocytes ]

:supplement[\\*specific antibodies have complementary shapes which fit specific antigens ]

:supplement[What happens during vaccination?]

- :supplement[weakened pathogens or their antigens are put into the body]
- :supplement[the antigens stimulate an immune response by lymphocytes which produce antibodies ]
- :supplement[memory cells are produced that give long term immunity ]

:supplement[(active immunity)]

:supplement[What is passive immunity?]

- :supplement[a short term defence against a pathogen by antibodies acquired from another individual, including across the placenta and in breast milk ]
- :supplement[memory cells are not produced]

:supplement[Why is breast feeding important?]

- :supplement[It allows for the development of passive immunity in infants ]

:supplement[Describe cholera:]

- :supplement[a disease caused by a bacterium which is transmitted in contaminated water]

:supplement[What is the effect of cholera?]

- :supplement[cholera bacterium produces a toxin that causes the secretion of chloride ions into the small intestine ]
- :supplement[this causes osmotic movement of water into the gut ]
- :supplement[causing diarrhoea, dehydration and loss of ions from the blood ]
