- :supplement[the movement of sucrose and amino acids in the phloem from sources to sinks]

:supplement[Describe sources:]

- :supplement[the parts of plants that release sucrose or amino acids]

:supplement[Describe sinks:]

- :supplement[The parts of plants that use or store sucrose or amino acids]

:supplement[Explain why some parts of a plant may act as a source and a sink at different times :]

:supplement[(example)]

- :supplement[an onion has stored carbohydrates in its bulb and acts as a source, the newly growing leaves use these carbohydrates to grow and act as a sink]
- :supplement[later when leaves start to grow from the bulb, they photosynthesise and release sugar acting as the source while the bulb will be the sink]
