:core[What is the function of the root hair?]

:core[What is the pathway taken by water from the soil to the leaf?]

- :core[root hair cell > root cortex cells > xylem vessel > mesophyll cells]

- :core[place a celery stalk in a beaker of water with blue dye]
- :core[after a few hours, remove the stalk and cut a few sections from the bottom]
