:supplement[Outline the functions of a nephron and the associated blood vessels:]

- :supplement[glomerulus: filtration of water, glucose, urea and ions from the blood (not protein as protein is too big to filter through - so there is no protein present in the nephron)]
- :supplement[nephron: reabsorption of all of the glucose, some of the ions, and most of the water back into the blood ]
- :supplement[forming urine containing urea, excess water, and excess ions (in the collecting duct)]

:supplement[What is the role of the liver:]

- :supplement[Amino acids are absorbed in the small intestine ]
- :supplement[Transported to the liver via the hepatic portal vein ]
- :supplement[In the liver, they are assimilated to form proteins]
- :supplement[Used for growth and repair ]

:supplement[Where is urea formed:]

- :supplement[formed in the liver from excess amino acids ]

:supplement[Describe deamination:]

- :supplement[the removal of the nitrogen containing part of amino acids to form urea]

:supplement[Why is excretion of urea important:]

- :supplement[urea is toxic when it accumulates in the blood]
- :supplement[it can damage cells or disrupt metabolic processes ]
- :supplement[therefore it is excreted to prevent poisoning of the body ]
