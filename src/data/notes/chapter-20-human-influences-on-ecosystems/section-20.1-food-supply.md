:core[How have humans increased food production?]

- :core[agricultural machinery: use larger areas of land and improve efficiency ]
- :core[chemical fertilisers: improve yields ]
- :core[insecticides: improve quality and yield ]
- :core[herbicides: reduce competition with weeds]
- :core[selective breeding: improve production by crop plants and livestock ]

:core[Describe the advantages and disadvantages of large scale monocultures:]

1. :core[advantages]

- :core[high yield ]
- :core[efficient ]
- :core[easier management ]

2. :core[disadvantages ]

- :core[lack of biodiversity ]
- :core[habitat loss ]
- :core[extinction of species ]
- :core[diseases spread easily ]
- :core[flooding/droughts ]
- :core[depletes soil nutrients ]
- :core[pollution by pesticides ]

:core[Describe the advantages and disadvantages of intensive livestock production:]

1. :core[advantages ]

- :core[more economical ]
- :core[less space used ]
- :core[more food produced ]
- :core[easier to monitor ]
- :core[efficient ]
- :core[protection from predators ]

2. :core[disadvantages ]

- :core[habitat loss]
- :core[overuse of antibiotics ]
- :core[animal welfare concerns ]
- :core[eutrophication/water pollution ]
- :core[higher greenhouse gas emissions ]
- :core[increased risk of disease ]
