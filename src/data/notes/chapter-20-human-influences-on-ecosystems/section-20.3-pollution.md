:core[What are the effects of untreated sewage and excess fertilisers on aquatic ecosystems?]

:core[\\*eutrophication]

- :core[Adds nutrients (nitrates/phosphates) to water ]

- :core[Causes rapid growth of algae ]
- :core[Light is blocked → aquatic plants die ]
- :core[Population of decomposers increase]
- :core[Decomposers use oxygen]
- :core[Oxygen levels decrease → fish and other organisms die]

:core[What are the effects of non biodegradable plastics in both aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems?]

- :core[Do not decompose (accumulates in environment) ]
- :core[Harm animals (ingestion can lead to choking/starvation) ]
- :core[Entanglement (can injure or kill organisms) ]
- :core[Pollute habitats (land and water) ]
- :core[Can enter food chains and bioaccumulate ]

1. :core[sources:]

- :core[carbon dioxide: burning fossil fuels, deforestation ]
- :core[methane: livestock, decaying organic waste materials (landfills)]

2. :core[effects: ]

- :core[enhanced greenhouse effect ]
- :core[climate change ]

- :supplement[increased availability of nitrate and other ions]
- :supplement[increased growth of producers ]
- :supplement[increased decomposition after death of producers ]
- :supplement[increased aerobic respiration by decomposers ]
