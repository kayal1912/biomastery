:supplement[What are the advantages and disadvantages of asexual reproduction?]

| **Advantages** | **Disadvantages** |
| --- | --- |
| No mate is needed | Little to no variation so unlikely to evolve or adapt to changing environments |
| No gametes are needed | If parent has no disease resistance, offspring won’t either |
| All the good characteristics of a parent are passed down to offspring | Lack of dispersal can lead to competition for nutrients, water, and light |
| No dispersal so offspring grow in same favourable conditions as parent plant |  |
