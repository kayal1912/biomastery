- :core[testosterone in development of secondary sexual characteristics:]

- :core[shoulders broaden ]
- :core[voice deepens]
- :core[sexual organs enlarge ]
- :core[facial hair grows]
- :core[growth of pubic hair]
- :core[increase in muscle/bone mass]
- :core[production/release of sperm]

- :core[oestrogen in development of secondary sexual characteristics:]

- :core[breasts grow]
- :core[hips widen ]
- :core[pubic hair grows ]
- :core[start of menstrual cycle ]

:core[Describe the menstrual cycle:]

- :core[Day 1-6: At the start of the cycle, the lining of the uterus breaks down and is shed (menstruation) ]
- :core[Day 6-13: The uterus lining then repairs and thickens ]
- :core[Day 14: In the ovary, an egg develops and is released (ovulation) ]
- :core[Day 15- 28: The uterus lining becomes thicker and develops more blood vessels ready for implantation ]
- :core[If fertilisation does not occur, the lining breaks down again]

:supplement[ Where are the following hormones produced?]

1. :supplement[oestrogen - ovaries ]
2. :supplement[progesterone - corpus luteum in the ovary; placenta during pregnancy ]

:supplement[Explain the roles of the following hormones:]

1. :supplement[FSH (follicle stimulating hormone)]

- :supplement[Stimulates development/growth/maturation of follicles in the ovary]
- :supplement[Stimulates development/growth/maturation of egg cells]
- :supplement[Stimulates production/secretion of oestrogen (from ovary)]

2. :supplement[LH (lutenising hormone)]

- :supplement[Stimulates ovulation (release of egg cell)]
- :supplement[Stimulates (corpus luteum) secretion of progesterone]

3. :supplement[Oestrogen]

- :supplement[Thickens the blood lining of the uterus ]

4. :supplement[Progesterone]

- :supplement[Maintains blood lining of uterus during pregnancy for implantation ]
- :supplement[Inhibits FSH and LH secretion]
