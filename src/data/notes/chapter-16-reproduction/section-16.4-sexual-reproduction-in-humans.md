:core[Describe the male reproductive system:]

![](/images/notes/image25.png)

| **Part** | **Function** |
| --- | --- |
| Penis | can become firm to insert into vagina of the female during sexual intercourse in order to transfer sperm |
| Prostate gland | adds fluid and nutrients to sperm to form semen |
| Scrotum | a sac that holds the testes outside the body keeping them cooler than body temperature |
| Sperm duct | muscular tube that links the testis to the urethra to allow the passage of semen containing sperm |
| Testis | male gonad that produces sperm |
| Urethra | passes semen containing sperm through the penis; also carries urine from the bladder |

:core[Describe the female reproductive system:]

![](/images/notes/image26.png)

| **Part** | **Function** |
| --- | --- |
| Cervix | a ring of muscle separating the vagina from the uterus |
| Ovary | female gonad, contains follicles in which ova are produced |
| Oviduct | carries an ovum to the uterus with propulsion provided by tiny cilia in the wall; site of fertilisation |
| Uterus | where the fetus develops |
| Vagina | receives the male penis during sexual intercourse |

:core[Describe fertilisation:]

- :core[the fusion of the nuclei from a male gamete (sperm) and a female gamete (egg cell)]

:core[Explain the adaptive features of sperm:]

- :core[flagellum: produces flicking movements to propel sperm and allow it to swim to egg cell for fertilisation ]
- :core[mitochondria: respires aerobically to release energy for movement to swim]
- :core[acrosome: has enzymes which digest the jelly coat of an egg cell to allow the sperm nucleus to enter for fertilisation ]

:core[Explain the adaptive features of egg cells:]

- :core[energy stores: has energy stores to provide energy for cell division and early development of an embryo ]
- :core[jelly coat: change after fertilisation to prevent more than one sperm entering]

:core[What are the differences between sperm and egg cells?]

| **Sperm** | **Egg cell** |
| --- | --- |
| Very small | Larger than sperm cell |
| Motile | Non-motile |
| Many sperm are produced | Very few egg cells are produced |

:core[What happens to the zygote after fertilisation?]

- :core[the zygote forms an embryo which is a ball of cells that implants into the lining of the uterus ]

:core[Identify the following in diagrams and state their functions :]![](/images/notes/image38.jpg)

| **Part** | **Function** |
| --- | --- |
| Umbilical cord | - keeps maternal and fetal blood separate - transfers oxygen and dissolved nutrients from mother to fetus - transfers excretory waste from fetus to mothers - transfers antibodies/passive immunity to fetus |
| Placenta | - secretes progesterone - forms barrier to toxins |
| Amniotic sac | - acts as barrier against infections - produces fluid and holds it - encloses fetus |
| Amniotic fluid | - absorbs shock - regulates temperature - allows lung development - allows fetus to move - muscle and bone developement |

- :core[oxygen ]

- :core[amino acids]
- :core[other nutrients (like vitamins)]

- :core[umbilical artery (from fetus)]

- :core[carbon dioxide ]
- :core[urea ]
