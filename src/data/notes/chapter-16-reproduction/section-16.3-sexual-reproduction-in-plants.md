:core[State the functions of the following structures:]

| **Part** | **Function** |
| --- | --- |
| Petal | large and colored to attract insects |
| Sepal | protects the flower while in bud |
| Stamen | male reproductive part of the flower, made up of anther and filament |
| Anther | contains pollen sacs in which pollen grains are formed |
| Filament | supports the anther |
| Carpel | female reproductive part of the flower, made up of stigma, style, and ovary |
| Stigma | a sticky surface that receives pollen during fertilization |
| Style | links the stigma to the ovary through which pollen tubes grow |
| Ovary | contains ovules |
| Ovule | contains a haploid nucleus, which develops into a seed when fertilised |

:core[List the features of wind and insect pollinated flowers:]

| **Wind** | **Insect** |
| --- | --- |
| Small/absent petals/flower | Large colorful petals to attract pollinators |
| Anther and filament hang freely outside the flower | Anthers and filament inside flower |
| Feathery stigma freely hanging outside flower | Stigma located inside flower |
| Small, smooth, lightweight pollen | large/heavy hooked/spiky pollen |

:core[Describe pollination:]

- :core[the transfer of pollen grains from an anther to a stigma ]

:core[When does fertilisation happen?]

- :core[fertilisation occurs when a pollen nucleus fuses with a nucleus in an ovule ]

:core[What environmental conditions are needed for germination?]

- :core[water]
- :core[oxygen ]
- :core[suitable temperature ]

:supplement[Describe self pollination:]

- :supplement[the transfer of pollen grains from the anther of a flower to the stigma of the same flower or a different flower on the same plant ]

:supplement[Describe cross pollination:]

- :supplement[the transfer of pollen grains from the anther of a flower to the stigma of a flower on a different plant of the same species ]

:supplement[What are the effects of self pollination and cross pollination?]

| **Self** | **Cross** |
| --- | --- |
| less genetic variation | more genetic variation |
| less likely to adapt to changing environment | more likely to adapt to changing environment |
| not reliant on pollinators | rely on pollinators |

:supplement[Describe the growth of a pollen tube:]

- :supplement[pollen grain lands on stigma ]
- :supplement[it absorbs liquid from the stigma and a microscopic pollen tube grows out of the grain ]
- :supplement[the tube grows down the style and into the ovary where it enters the ovule]
- :supplement[it enters the ovule through the micropyle and fertilisation occurs ]
