:supplement[What is the significance of villi and microvilli:]

- :supplement[they increase the internal surface of the small intestine for diffusion ]
- :supplement[they have thin epithelium to decrease diffusion rate]
- :supplement[they have a dense network of blood capillaries for good blood supply ]
- :supplement[they have lacteals to absorb fat]

:supplement[Describe the roles of capillaries:]

- :supplement[Absorbs nutrients into the bloodstream and carries them away quickly ]
- :supplement[maintains a steep concentration gradient for faster, more efficient absorption]

:supplement[Describe the roles of lacteals:]

- :supplement[Absorbs fatty acids and glycerol ]
- :supplement[Transports them through the lymphatic system ]
- :supplement[Helps maintain a concentration gradient for fat absorption]
