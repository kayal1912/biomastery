:core[Describe physical digestion:]

- :core[the breakdown of food into smaller pieces without chemical change to the food molecules ]

:core[What is the role of physical digestion?]

- :core[it increases the surface area of food for the action of enzymes in chemical digestion ]

:core[Describe the functions of the types of human teeth in physical digestion:]

- :core[incisor: biting off pieces of food]
- :core[canine: similar function to incisors]
- :core[premolar: tearing and grinding food ]
- :core[molar: chewing and grinding food]

:core[What is the function of the stomach in physical digestion?]

- :core[The muscular walls of the stomach contract and relax (peristalsis) to churn and mix the food with gastric juices ]
- :core[This breaks the food into smaller pieces ]
- :core[And forms a creamy liquid mixture called chyme]

:supplement[What is the role of bile in physical digestion?]

- :supplement[it emulsifies fats and oils into small droplets to increase surface area for chemical digestion by enzymes ]
