:core[Describe a balanced diet:]

- :core[A diet containing all essential nutrients in the correct proportions]

:core[State the importance and main dietary sources of the following nutrients:]

| **Nutrient** | **Dietary source** | **Importance** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbohydrates | Bread, rice, potato | Source of energy; Storage |
| Fats and oils | Butter, milk, cheese | Source of energy; Insulation; Cell membranes; Some hormones |
| Proteins | Meat, fish, eggs | Growth; Tissue repair; Enzymes; Some hormones; Cell membranes; Hair & nails |
| Vitamin C | Citrus fruits, tomato, potato | Prevents scurvy |
| Vitamin D | Butter, milk, fish-liver oil | Prevents rickets |
| Calcium | Milk, cheese, fish | Needed for healthy bones and for normal blood clotting |
| Iron | Red meat, liver, eggs | Needed for formation of haemoglobin |
| Fibre (roughage) | Vegetables and other plant material | Increases contents of the colon and helps to retain water; Softens faeces |
| Water |  | Used as a solvent |

:core[What is scurvy?]

- :core[a disease caused by the lack of Vitamin C]

:core[What are the symptoms of scurvy?]

- :core[bleeding gums ]
- :core[loose teeth ]
- :core[poor wound healing ]
- :core[fatigue / weakness]
- :core[bruising easily]

:core[What ]:core[is rickets]:core[?]

- :core[a disease caused by the lack of Vitamin D]

:core[What are the symptoms of rickets?]

- :core[soft/weak bones]
- :core[bone deformities (bowed legs)]
- :core[bone pain]
- :core[stunted growth in children ]
