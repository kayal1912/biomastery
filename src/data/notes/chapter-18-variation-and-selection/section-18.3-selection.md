:core[Describe the process of natural selection:]

- :core[there is genetic variation within populations ]
- :core[there is selection pressure (change in the environment) ]
- :core[there is a struggle for survival and competition for resources ]

- :core[individuals with advantageous features are more likely to survive and reproduce]

- :core[these (advantageous) individuals pass on their alleles to the next generation  ]

:core[Describe the process of selective breeding (4 marks - question will be asked through an example):]

- :core[humans select individuals with desirable features ]
- :core[these individuals are crossed to produce the next generation ]
- :core[the offspring with the desired features are selected]
- :core[the process is repeated over many generations ]

:supplement[Describe adaptation:]

- :supplement[the process, resulting from natural selection, by which populations become more suited to their environment over many generations]

:supplement[What is an example of natural selection?]

- :supplement[development of strains of antibiotic resistant bacteria ]

- :supplement[a mutation occurs]
- :supplement[some bacteria become resistant to antibiotics ]
- :supplement[the antibiotics kill all the non resistant bacteria ]
- :supplement[the resistant bacteria survive and reproduce ]
- :supplement[they pass on their genes for resistance to the next generation of offspring ]

:supplement[What are the differences between natural and artificial selection?]

| **Natural** | **Artificial** |
| --- | --- |
| features are adaptive | humans choose desired features |
| caused by environment | selection not influenced by environment |
| competition between individuals | humans choose individuals (for economic/aesthetic reasons) |
| slower | faster |
| maintains variation | decreases variation |
