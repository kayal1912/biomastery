:core[Describe an adaptive feature:]

- :core[an inherited feature that helps an organism to survive and reproduce in its environment ]

:supplement[What are the adaptive features of:]

1. :supplement[Hydrophytes]

- :supplement[aerenchyma: provides buoyancy ]
- :supplement[thin cuticle: no need to prevent water loss]
- :supplement[many stomata on upper surface only: exposed to air, prevents water from entering the leaf, allows for high rate of transpiration ]

2. :supplement[Xerophytes]

- :supplement[thick waxy cuticle: reduced water loss]
- :supplement[sunken stomata: traps humid air, reduces transpiration ]
- :supplement[leaves reduced to spines: less surface area for transpiration ]
- :supplement[rolled leaves: traps moisture, reducing transpiration]
- :supplement[extensive roots: maximise water uptake ]
