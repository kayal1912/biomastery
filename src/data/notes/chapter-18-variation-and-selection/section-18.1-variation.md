:core[Describe variation:]

- :core[the differences between individuals of the same species ]

:core[What is continuous variation?]

- :core[characteristics as a result of a range of phenotypes between two extremes (like height and body mass)]
- :core[caused by both genes and environment ]

:core[What is discontinuous variation?]

- :core[characteristics as a result of a limited number of phenotypes with no intermediates (like ABO blood groups, seed shape and seed color in peas)]
- :core[caused by genes only]

:core[Describe mutation:]

- :core[a genetic change]

:core[How are new alleles formed?]

- :core[mutation]

:core[What factors increase the rate of mutation?]

- :core[ionising radiation ]
- :core[some chemicals ]
