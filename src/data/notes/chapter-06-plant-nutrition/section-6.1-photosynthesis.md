:core[Define photosynthesis:]

- :core[the process by which plants synthesise carbohydrates from raw materials using energy from light ]

:core[State the word equation for photosynthesis:]

- :core[carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen]

:core[(in the presence of light and chlorophyll)]

:core[What is chlorophyll and where is it found?]

- :core[it’s a green pigment ]
- :core[that is found in chloroplasts ]

:core[What does chlorophyll do?]

- :core[It absorbs light energy and transfers it to chemical energy]

:core[What are the carbohydrates made in photosynthesis used/stored as?]

- :core[starch: energy store]
- :core[cellulose: build cell walls]
- :core[glucose: respiration to provide energy ]
- :core[sucrose: transport in the phloem ]
- :core[nectar: attract insects for pollination ]

:core[Why are nitrate ions important?]

- :core[needed to make amino acids ]

:core[Why are magnesium ions important?]

- :core[needed to make chlorophyll ]

:core[What are the factors needed for photosynthesis to take place? ]

- :core[light ]
- :core[carbon dioxide ]
- :core[water]
- :core[chlorophyll ]

:core[What factors increase the rate of photosynthesis?]

- :core[warm temperature (too high will denature enzymes in the plant)]
- :core[high carbon dioxide concentration ]
- :core[high light intensity ]

- :core[low concentration: purple]
- :core[normal concentration: red]
- :core[high concentration: yellow ]
