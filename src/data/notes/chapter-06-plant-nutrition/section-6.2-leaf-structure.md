:core[How are leaves adapted for photosynthesis?]

- :core[they have a large surface area: allow more light to be absorbed and provide more area for gas exchange ]
- :core[they are thin: provides short diffusion distance for carbon dioxide and allows more light to penetrate the leaf]

:core[Explain how the following structures in leaves are adapted for photosynthesis:]

- :core[Chloroplasts: contain chlorophyll to absorb light energy for photosynthesis ]
- :core[Cuticle: thin and transparent, allowing light to pass through ]
- :core[Guard cells and stomata: allow gas exchange (CO₂ enters, O₂ exits); guard cells control opening and closing of stomata ]
- :core[Upper epidermis: thin and transparent to let light reach mesophyll cells ]
- :core[Lower epidermis: contains stomata for gas exchange ]
- :core[Palisade mesophyll: packed with many chloroplasts to absorb maximum light ]
- :core[Spongy mesophyll: loosely packed with air spaces for gas exchange ]
- :core[Air spaces: allow rapid diffusion of gases (CO₂ and O₂) ]
- :core[Vascular bundles: transport substances and provide support ]
- :core[Xylem: transports water to the leaf for photosynthesis ]
- :core[Phloem: transports sugars (glucose) away from the leaf]
