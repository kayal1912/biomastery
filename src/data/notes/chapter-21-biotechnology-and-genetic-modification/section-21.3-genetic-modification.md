:core[Describe genetic modification:]

- :core[changing the genetic material of an organism by removing, changing or inserting individual genes ]

:core[What are some examples of genetic modification?]

- :core[insertion of human genes into bacteria to produce human proteins ]
- :core[insertion of genes into crop plants to confer resistance to herbicides ]
- :core[insertion of genes into crop plants to confer resistance to insect pests ]
- :core[insertion of genes into crop plants to improve nutritional qualities ]

:supplement[Outline the process of genetic modification using bacteria and human genes:]

- :supplement[the DNA making up a human gene is isolated using a restriction enzyme forming sticky ends ]
- :supplement[the bacterial plasmid DNA is cut with the same restriction enzyme, forming complementary sticky ends]
- :supplement[the human DNA is inserted into the bacterial plasmid DNA using DNA lipase to form a recombinant plasmid ]
- :supplement[the recombinant plasmid is placed back into the bacteria ]
- :supplement[the bacteria containing the recombinant plasmid multiplies ]
- :supplement[the human gene is expressed in the bacteria and the human protein is produced ]

:supplement[What are the advantages and disadvantages of genetically modifying crops?]

1. :supplement[Advantages:]

- :supplement[Increased yield ]
- :supplement[Resistance to pests/diseases meaning less pesticide use ]
- :supplement[Tolerance to harsh conditions (like drought) ]
- :supplement[Improved nutritional value ]

2. :supplement[Disadvantages: ]

- :supplement[Possible harm to ecosystems (like gene transfer to wild plants) ]
- :supplement[Reduction in biodiversity]
- :supplement[Ethical concerns ]
- :supplement[Uncertain long-term health effects]
