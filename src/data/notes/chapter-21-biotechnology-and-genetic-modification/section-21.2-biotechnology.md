:core[Describe the use of biological washing powders:]

- :core[Biological washing powders contain enzymes (like proteases, lipases, amylases) ]
- :core[These enzymes break down stains such as protein, fat, and starch ]
- :core[This breaks down the stains into soluble substances so they can be washed away]
- :core[They work effectively at low/medium temperatures, as temperatures too high would lead to denaturation of the enzymes ]
