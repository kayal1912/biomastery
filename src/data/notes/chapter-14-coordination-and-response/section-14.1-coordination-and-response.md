:core[How do electrical impulses travel?]

- :core[travel along neurones ]

:core[Describe the mammalian nervous system:]

1. :core[central nervous system (CNS) - brain and spinal chord ]
2. :core[peripheral nervous system (PNS) - nerves outside the brain and spinal chord ]

:core[What is the role of the nervous system?]

- :core[Coordination and regulation of body functions ]

:core[Describe a reflex arc:]

- :core[receptor (sense organ) → sensory neurone → relay neurone → motor neurone → effector (muscles or glands)]

:core[Describe a synapse:]

- :core[a junction between two neurones ]

:supplement[Describe the structure of a synapse:]

![](/images/notes/image7.jpg)

:supplement[Describe the events at a synapse:]

- :supplement[an impulse stimulates the release of neurotransmitter molecules from vesicles into the synaptic gap ]
- :supplement[the neurotransmitter molecules diffuse across the gap ]
- :supplement[neurotransmitter molecules bind with receptor proteins on the membrane of the next neurone ]
- :supplement[an impulse is stimulated in the next neurone ]

:supplement[What is the role of a synapse?]

- :supplement[synapses ensure that impulses travel in one direction only ]
