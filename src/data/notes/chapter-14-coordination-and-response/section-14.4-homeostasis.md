:supplement[Explain the concept of homeostatic control:]

- :supplement[A set point is the normal/optimum level (like body temperature)]
- :supplement[In negative feedback, if conditions deviate from the set point, the body triggers responses to reverse the change]
- :supplement[When the set point is restored, the response is stopped  
  ]

:supplement[Describe the control of blood glucose concentration:]

1. :supplement[High blood glucose levels]

1. :supplement[high glucose levels detected by the pancreas]
2. :supplement[pancreas releases ]:supplement[**insulin**]:supplement[ ]
3. :supplement[insulin stimulates the liver to convert glucose into glycogen and store it]
4. :supplement[therefore reducing the blood glucose levels to the set point ]

2. :supplement[Low blood glucose levels]

1. :supplement[low glucose levels detected by the pancreas]
2. :supplement[pancreas releases ]:supplement[**glucagon**]:supplement[ ]
3. :supplement[glucagon stimulates the liver to convert glycogen back to glucose and release it into the blood stream ]
4. :supplement[therefore increasing blood glucose levels to the set point]

:supplement[Describe maintenance of a constant internal body temperature:]

:supplement[\\*body temp is maintained through homeostasis and negative feedback]

- :supplement[when cold]

- :supplement[vasoconstriction: the arterioles in the dermis constrict allowing less blood to flow near the skin hence reducing heat loss to the environment]
- :supplement[hair erector muscles contract and the hairs stand up, trapping a layer of air near the skin as insulation ]
- :supplement[shivering: muscles in the body rapidly contract to carry out more respiration and release more energy for heat ]

- :supplement[when hot  ]

- :supplement[vasodilation: the arterioles in the dermis dilate allowing more blood to flow near the skin hence increasing heat loss to the environment ]
- :supplement[sweating: sweat glands secrete sweat, when the sweat evaporates it absorbs energy from the skin causing the skin to feel cooler ]
- :supplement[fat layer under the skin serves as insulation ]
