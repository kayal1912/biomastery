:core[ negative phototropism - away from light source]

- :core[shoot: positive phototropism, negative gravitropism ]

- :supplement[it grows towards light for photosynthesis and away from the ground (up) ]

- :core[root: negative phototropism, positive gravitropism ]

- :supplement[it grows away from light towards the ground]

:supplement[Explain the role of auxin in controlling shoot growth ( for example towards light ):]

- :supplement[auxin is made in the shoot tip]
- :supplement[auxin diffuses through the plant from the shoot tip (to the shady side of the plant)]
- :supplement[auxin is unequally distributed in response to light and gravity ]
- :supplement[auxin stimulates cell elongation (the cells elongate on the shady side and the plant bends toward light)]
