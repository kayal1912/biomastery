:core[Explain pupil reflex:]

- :core[when in bright light, the pupil diameter decreases allowing less light to enter the eye]
- :core[when in dim light, the pupil diameter increases allowing more light to enter the eye]

:supplement[Explain pupil reflex:]

:supplement[(an automatic response to light intensity)]

- :supplement[in bright light]

- :supplement[the circular muscles of the iris contract and the radial muscles relax (they act antagonistically)]
- :supplement[pupil size decreases / the iris widens]
- :supplement[allowing less light to enter the eye]

- :supplement[in dim light]

- :supplement[the radial muscles contract and the circular muscles relax]
- :supplement[pupil size increases]
- :supplement[allowing more light to enter the eye]

:supplement[Explain accommodation for viewing near and distant objects:]

1. :supplement[near objects (4 marks) ]

1. :supplement[ciliary muscles contract]
2. :supplement[tension in suspensory ligaments decreases ]
3. :supplement[lens becomes more convex/fatter]
4. :supplement[refracting more light ]

2. :supplement[distant objects]

1. :supplement[ciliary muscles relax ]
2. :supplement[suspensory ligaments tighten ]
3. :supplement[suspensory ligaments pull on/stretch the lens]
4. :supplement[lens becomes thinner/less convex]
5. :supplement[less light is refracted]

:supplement[Describe the distribution of rods and cones in the retina (3 marks):]

- :supplement[they are found in the retina]
- :supplement[more rods than cones (uneven distribution)]
- :supplement[the highest concentration of cones are found in the fovea, no rods in the fovea]
- :supplement[there are no rods or cones at the blind spot/in front of the optic nerve]

:supplement[What are the functions of rods and cones ?]

- :supplement[they are both light receptors]

- :supplement[rods: ]

- :supplement[detect low light intensities]
- :supplement[give night vision]
- :supplement[give greyscale image]

- :supplement[cones:]

- :supplement[detect high light intensities]
- :supplement[detect color ]
- :supplement[3 types of cones that absorb different wavelengths of light (red, green, and blue)]

:supplement[Identify in diagrams and state the function of the fovea:]

- :supplement[the fovea is the region of the retina where the sharpest images are formed]
- :supplement[there is a very high concentration of cone cells in the fovea, no rods present]
- :supplement[when observing and object up close, you are making the image fall on the fovea]
