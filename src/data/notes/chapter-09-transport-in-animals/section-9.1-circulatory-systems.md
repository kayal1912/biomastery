:core[Describe the circulatory system:]

- :core[a system of blood vessels with a pump and valves to ensure one way flow of blood ]

:supplement[Describe the single circulation of a fish:]

- :supplement[Blood passes through the heart once in each complete circuit ]
- :supplement[Blood moves from the heart to the gills where it gets oxygenated ]
- :supplement[Then the oxygenated blood moves to the body muscles before returning back to the heart ]

:supplement[Describe the double circulation of a mammal:]

- :supplement[Blood passes through the heart twice in each complete circuit ]
- :supplement[The blood moves from the heart to the lungs where it gets oxygenated]
- :supplement[The oxygenated blood moves back to the heart before being pumped to the rest of the body tissues ]

:supplement[Explain the advantages of a double circulation (according to the mark scheme):]

- :supplement[keeps oxygenated and deoxygenated blood separate]
- :supplement[ensures efficient supply of oxygen (to body)]
- :supplement[low pressure in pulmonary artery (to lungs)]

- :supplement[(to) prevent damage to capillaries in the lungs]

- :supplement[allows more time for gas exchange ]
- :supplement[allows higher blood pressure to body ]
- :supplement[allows filtration in kidneys]
- :supplement[maintain a high metabolic rate/rate of respiration ]
