:core[ What is coronary heart disease (CHD)?]

- :core[the blockage of the coronary arteries ]

:core[What are the risk factors of CHD?]

- :core[diet ]
- :core[lack of exercise ]
- :core[age ]
- :core[sex]
- :core[stress]
- :core[smoking ]
- :core[genetic predisposition]

:core[What is the role of diet and exercise in reducing the risk of coronary heart disease?]

1. :core[Diet: ]

- :core[Reduce saturated fats/cholesterol → lowers risk of fatty deposits in arteries ]
- :core[Increase fibre intake → helps lower blood cholesterol ]
- :core[Reduce salt → lowers blood pressure ]
- :core[Avoid excess calories → prevents obesity (risk factor) ]

2. :core[Exercise: ]

- :core[Strengthens the heart muscle → more efficient pumping ]
- :core[Helps maintain healthy body weight → lessens strain on heart ]
- :core[Lowers blood pressure  
  ]

:supplement[Explain the relative thickness of the muscle walls of the left ventricle:]

- :supplement[the left ventricle wall is thicker than that of the right ventricle ]
- :supplement[because right ventricle only needs to pump blood to the lungs with low pressure]
- :supplement[while the left ventricle needs to pump blood to the whole body with high pressure]

:supplement[Explain the relative thickness of the atria walls compared to ventricle walls:]

- :supplement[atria have thinner walls than ventricles ]
- :supplement[because atria only pump blood a short distance into the ventricles as low pressure]
- :supplement[ventricles need to pump blood out of the heart at high pressure to other organs  
  ]

:supplement[Explain the importance of the septum:]

- :supplement[the septum separates the left and right sides of the heart ]
- :supplement[this prevents mixing of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood ]
- :supplement[so the body receives fully oxygenated blood at high pressure → more efficient respiration]

:supplement[Describe the functioning of the heart:]

- :supplement[deoxygenated blood from the vena cava enters the right atrium]
- :supplement[the right atrium contracts, pumping the blood to the right ventricle, the atrioventricular valves are open ]
- :supplement[the right ventricle then contracts and pumps the blood to the lungs in the pulmonary artery, the atrioventricular valve closes to prevent blood flowing back into the right atrium and the semilunar valve is open]
- :supplement[when the ventricles relax, the semilunar valves close]
- :supplement[the left atrium receives oxygenated blood from the pulmonary vein, it contracts and pumps the blood to the left ventricle, the atrioventricular valve is open ]
- :supplement[the left ventricle contracts, the atrioventricular valve closes and semilunar valve opens, pumping the blood through the aorta to the rest of the body ]

:supplement[Explain the effect of physical activity on the heart rate:]

- :supplement[physical activity increases heart rate]
- :supplement[more energy is required by the muscles for muscle contraction during physical activity]
- :supplement[the heart pumps blood faster to deliver more oxygen / glucose to increase rate of aerobic respiration]
- :supplement[adrenaline is also released during physical activity which causes the heart rate to elevate]
- :supplement[it also helps in the removal of carbon dioxide from the blood]
