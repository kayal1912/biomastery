:core[Describe the structure of arteries, veins, and capillaries:]

| **Blood vessel** | **Artery** | **Vein** | **Capilary** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Relative thickness of wall** | Thick walls | Thin walls | Very thin walls (only one cell thick) |
| **Diameter of lumen** | Narrow lumen | Wide lumen | Approximately one red blood cell wide |
| **Valves** | Absent | Present | Absent |

:core[What is the function of capillaries?]

- :core[Walls are one cell thick → short diffusion distance ]
- :core[Form a large network (large surface area) → more exchange ]
- :core[Slow blood flow → more time for diffusion ]
- :core[Allow diffusion of oxygen and glucose into cells ]
- :core[Allow diffusion of carbon dioxide and other wastes into blood  
  ]

:supplement[Arteries: ]

- :supplement[Thick, muscular and elastic walls → withstand high pressure ]
- :supplement[Elastic fibres → stretch and recoil to maintain pressure ]
- :supplement[Narrow lumen → keeps pressure high ]
- :supplement[No valves → pressure is high enough to maintain one way blood flow]

:supplement[Veins:]

- :supplement[Thin walls with less muscle/elastic tissue → blood at low pressure ]
- :supplement[Wide lumen → reduces resistance to blood flow ]
- :supplement[Valves present → prevent backflow (since pressure is low)  
  ]

:supplement[How do the structures of capillaries relate to their function:]

- :supplement[Walls one cell thick → very short diffusion distance for rapid exchange ]
- :supplement[Permeable walls (gaps) → allow plasma and dissolved substances to pass out ]
- :supplement[Very narrow lumen → red blood cells pass single file, closer to walls → faster diffusion ]
- :supplement[Form a large network (large surface area) → more area for exchange of substances]
- :supplement[Slow blood flow → more time for diffusion  
  ]
