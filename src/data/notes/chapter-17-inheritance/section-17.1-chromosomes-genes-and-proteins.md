:core[Define gene:]

- :core[a length of DNA that codes for protein]

:core[Define allele:]

- :core[an alternative form of a gene]

- :core[Females have XX chromosomes ]
- :core[Males have XY chromosomes]
- :core[Gametes each contain one sex chromosome ]
- :core[Egg cells always carry an X chromosome ]
- :core[Sperm cells always carry either an X or Y chromosome]
- :core[Fertilization with an X sperm produces an XX female zygote ]
- :core[Fertilization with a Y sperm produces an XY male zygote ]

- :supplement[the sequence of bases in a gene determine the sequence of amino acids used for making a specific protein ]

:supplement[How are the shapes of specific proteins determined?]

- :supplement[the sequence of amino acids determines the shape and type of a specific protein ]

:supplement[How does DNA control ]:supplement[cell]:supplement[ function?]

:supplement[Explain how protein is made:]

- :supplement[the gene coding for the protein remains in the nucleus ]
- :supplement[messenger RNA (mRNA) is a copy of a gene]
- :supplement[mRNA molecules are made in the nucleus and move to the cytoplasm ]
- :supplement[the mRNA passes through ribosomes ]
- :supplement[the ribosome assembles amino acids into protein molecules ]
- :supplement[the specific sequence of amino acids is determined by the sequence of bases in the mRNA ]

:supplement[Why do body cells have different functions though they contain the same genes?]

- :supplement[most cells in an organism contain the same genes]
- :supplement[but many genes in a particular cell are not expressed because the cell only makes the specific proteins it needs ]

:supplement[Describe haploid nucleus:]

- :supplement[a nucleus containing a single set of chromosomes ]

:supplement[Describe diploid nucleus:]

- :supplement[a nucleus containing two sets of chromosomes ]

:supplement[How many chromosomes are in a diploid cell?]

- :supplement[there is a pair of each type of chromosome]
