:core[Describe inheritance: ]

- :core[the transmission of genetic information from generation to generation ]

:core[Describe genotype:]

- :core[the genetic make-up of an organism ]

:core[Describe phenotype: ]

- :core[the observable features of an organism ]

:core[Describe homozygous:]

- :core[having two identical alleles of a particular gene ]

:core[\\*when two homozygous individuals breed it will be pure-breeding ]

:core[Describe heterozygous:]

- :core[having two different alleles for a particular gene ]

:core[\\*when a heterozygous individual breeds it will not be pure breeding ]

:core[Describe a dominant allele:]

- :core[an allele that is expressed if it is present in the genotype ]

:core[Describe recessive allele:]

- :core[an allele that is only expressed when there is no dominant allele of the gene present in the genotype ]
