:core[Describe a population:]

- :core[a group of organisms of one species, living in the same area, at the same time ]

:core[Describe a community:]

- :core[all of the populations of different species in an ecosystem ]

:core[Describe ecosystem:]

- :core[a unit containing the community of organisms and their environment interacting together ]

:core[What factors increase rate of population growth:]

- :core[increased food supply ]
- :core[less competition ]
- :core[less predation ]
- :core[less disease]

:supplement[Explain the factors that lead to each phase in the sigmoid curve:]

- :supplement[at log phase, the reproduction rate is greater than the death rate; this could be due to increase in food supply, or decrease in disease/predation/competition]
- :supplement[at stationary phase, the reproduction rate is equal to the death rate; this could be a result of increased disease/predation/competition, or decrease in food supply ]
- :supplement[at death phase, the reproduction rate is lower than the death rate and population decreases ]
