:core[Describe anaerobic respiration:]

- :core[the chemical reactions in cells that break down nutrient molecules to release energy without using oxygen ]

:core[Which type of respiration releases more energy?]

- :core[Aerobic respiration releases much more energy per glucose molecule than anaerobic respiration ]

:core[What is the word equation for anaerobic respiration in yeast:]

- :core[Glucose → alcohol + carbon dioxide]

:core[What is the word equation for anaerobic respiration in muscles during vigorous exercise?]

- :core[glucose → lactic acid]

:supplement[What is the balanced chemical equation for anaerobic respiration in yeast?]

- :supplement[C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂]

:supplement[What happens during anaerobic respiration in the muscles?]

- :supplement[Lactic acid builds up in muscles and blood during vigorous exercise ]
- :supplement[Causing an oxygen debt ]

:supplement[How is oxygen debt removed:]

- :supplement[continuation of fast heart rate to transport lactic acid in the blood form the muscles to the liver ]
- :supplement[continuation of deeper and faster breathing to supply oxygen for aerobic respiration of lactic acid in the liver]
- :supplement[aerobic respiration of lactic acid in the liver ]
