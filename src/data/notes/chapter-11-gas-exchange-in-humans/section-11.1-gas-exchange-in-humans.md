:supplement[Explain the ventilation of the lungs:]

:supplement[During inhalation]

- :supplement[the diaphragm contracts ]
- :supplement[external intercostal muscles contract ]
- :supplement[internal intercostal muscles relax]
- :supplement[the ribs move upward and outward ]
- :supplement[air moves into lungs]
- :supplement[the volume of the lungs increases]
- :supplement[the pressure in the lungs decreases ]

:supplement[\\*pressure and volume are inversely proportional]

:supplement[During exhalation]

- :supplement[the diaphragm relaxes ]
- :supplement[external intercostal muscles relax]
- :supplement[internal intercostal muscles contract]
- :supplement[the ribs move inward and down]
- :supplement[air pushed out of lungs]
- :supplement[the volume decreases]
- :supplement[pressure in lungs increases ]

:supplement[How does physical activity increase breathing rate and depth:]

- :supplement[is detected by the brain ]
- :supplement[leading to an increased rate and greater depth of breathing ]
- :supplement[(to supply more oxygen for respiration and remove carbon dioxide)]
