:core[Outline the roles of water in the organisms:]

- :core[Water is a solvent in digestion of nutrients, excretion of metabolic waste, and transport of substances ]

:core[How does water diffuse:]

- :core[water diffuses through a partially permeable membrane by osmosis ]

:core[How does water enter a cell?]

- :core[water moves into and out of cells by osmosis through the cell membrane ]

:core[Describe the effect of immersing a plant cell in the following solutions:]

1. :core[Isotonic solution - no change]
2. :core[Hypotonic solution - water enters cell by osmosis; cell swells up / gets larger]
3. :core[Hypertonic - water leaves cell by osmosis; cell shrinks / shrivels up]

:core[How are plants supported?]

- :core[plants are supported by the pressure of water inside the cells pressing outwards on the cell wall]
